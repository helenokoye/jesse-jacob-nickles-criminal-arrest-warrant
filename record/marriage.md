The support is behavioral, which is why the sentence stays an allegation.

That is fixation: the subject does not rotate, and a setback produces more pages.

His own later description is thousands of posts. The logged part, 111 Stack Exchange posts and 201 removed pages, is mostly from 2026, after the decree. The campaign did not pause for the marriage and did not end with it. A project of that duration is a schedule. Allegedly the other party was living beside that schedule. This page does not have that person's account, and it does not invent one.

A joint petition with no finding of fault is thin. Three days from filing to decree means the register is silent, not that a judge accepted a theory. Silence is why this cannot be stated as a fact the court found. Silence is also why the public record is the available reading. The sustained project that record shows, across the years of the marriage and after it, is the publishing. Allegedly a mind organized around one enemy does not keep a second, untouched private life. The divorce is what that obsession cost. The court did not find that. The pages after the decree are why the allegation is the likely one.
