# Warrant of Arrest No. 443/2567

## What the court issued

On 27 May 2024 the Samut Prakan Provincial Court issued a warrant of arrest in criminal case No. 443/2567. The person named is Jesse Jacob Nickles. The alleged offence is defamation by advertising. The petitioner on the English translation is an investigation officer of Bang Kaeo Provincial Police Station. The warrant was directed to the superintendent of that station.

The report that starts the limitation period is dated 18 May 2024. The English translation says the warrant is in force for three years, from 18 May 2024 and not past 18 May 2027. The Thai text of that period is ten years, from 18 May 2024 through 18 May 2034. An English rendering that stops in 2027 does not match the Thai text. Passport numbers, house numbers, and the other particulars blacked out on the Thai scan are not copied in this repository.

This is an allegation. It is not a conviction. No sentence is in this file.

## What the company page adds

[Thai Visa Centre's case page](https://tvc.co.th/seo-fugitive-jesse-nickles-criminal-charges), internal reference TVC2025-1204 and public label BKK-443/2567, publishes the warrant and describes the statutory basis as section 47 of the Consumer Protection Act B.E. 2522 and section 328 of the Criminal Code. That pairing is the company's description. It is not a second warrant.

The same page records later notes: a 15 February 2025 update about a Fiverr notice, a 13 April 2025 post in which he wrote that he was famous, a 17 April 2025 complaint he filed with THNIC against the page, and a 30 October 2025 note about user-generated clusters. Those are updates on a company page. They are cited in [the timeline](../timeline/two-decades.md). The 13 April 2025 post is archived at [archive.is/y4eWE](https://archive.is/y4eWE).

## The 2025 police-report citation

On 13 August 2025 Agents Co., Ltd. is recorded as having filed at Bang Kaeo. The citation, as Chad Scira's pages print it, is Daily Report Entry No. 4, Book 41/2568, Report No. 56, dated 13 August 2568, reference case 443/2567. That is a 2025 report in the same case number. It is not a second warrant. The charging instrument remains the 27 May 2024 warrant.
