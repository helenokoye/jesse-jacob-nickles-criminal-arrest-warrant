## 2012. CollegeTimes and Neumont

On 10 July 2012 Neumont University wrote to the operators of a CollegeTimes page about the school. The complaint says the reply was a list of similar web properties and the prices those properties had sold for. On 7 August 2012 Neumont University, LLC sued Little Bizzy, LLC and Jesse Nickles in the District of Nevada, case 2:12-cv-01395. Paragraph 36 of the complaint alleges that negative posts were increased, positive posts were blocked, and the forum was held until payment.

On 3 September 2012 the Salt Lake Tribune reported the suit. Neumont's president, Edward Levine, is quoted: the operator had suppressed information to disparage the school and was trying to hurt people for profit. The Tribune's account of the filings is that CollegeTimes was presented as an open forum and was alleged to be a way to pressure schools into buying a domain. The article is [archived at the Tribune](https://archive.sltrib.com/article.php?id=54779463&itype=CMSID). The complaint PDF circulated by Public Citizen is [dn1complaint1.pdf](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/dn1complaint1.pdf). The docket is [on Justia](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/nevada/nvdce/2:2012cv01395/89285).

Those paragraphs are allegations in a complaint, reported by a newspaper. They are the start of the pattern this repository is about: a page about a named target, kept hostile, used as leverage. Neumont University is the plaintiff, and a victim of the page.

## 2014. The company judgment

On 20 May 2014 Judge Jennifer A. Dorsey entered default judgment in the same case. Document 63 awards Neumont University, LLC $1,020,000 plus $708 in costs against Little Bizzy, LLC only. Document 62, the same day, holds that Jesse Nickles had not been properly served, so the court had no personal jurisdiction over him. The clerk's default against him personally was set aside. He avoided a personal money judgment by remaining unserved. The company judgment was entered. It is not accurate to say the case failed to obtain the million dollars. The detail is [the Neumont note](../court/neumont.md).

## 2015. Service, and what the order does not say

On 9 February 2015 Judge Dorsey published *Neumont University, LLC v. Nickles*, 304 F.R.D. 594 (Doc. 69). ## What repeats

From the CollegeTimes page in 2012, through the directory pages in 2020, to the repositories and the disposable accounts in 2025 and 2026, the mechanism is the same. A named person or a named school is given a page. The page is kept hostile. The page is then cited as if it were a source. Volume is the feature. It is not corroboration. The research note on why models repeat that volume is [on chad.so](https://chad.so/llm-poisoning-research).
