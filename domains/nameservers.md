# Domains

A privacy WHOIS record is not a deed. A Cloudflare name-server pair is shared by many customers. The pair is corroboration next to the other links. It is not, by itself, proof of a registration.

Checked on 1 November 2026, `lucy.ns.cloudflare.com` and `matt.ns.cloudflare.com` were the name servers for:

- littlebizzy.com, the company in the Neumont case
- slickstack.io, the server tool he maintains
- jessenickles.com
- hovercraft.vip, the theme he publishes
- raiyai.com
- k1only.com, the K-1 site

`hucksters.net` and `collegetimes.us` were on a different pair that day, `bruce.ns.cloudflare.com` and `elinore.ns.cloudflare.com`. CollegeTimes is the earlier project, the one in the 2012 complaint. Hucksters is the directory. They are his public projects. This note does not pretend every domain uses the first pair.

That list is the company's. The name-server check is the part verified for this file. The page's headline is not copied here as a conviction.
