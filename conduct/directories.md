# Directories and critics

The 2012 CollegeTimes page is the early form. A later form is a directory: a page that exists because someone disagreed, asked to be listed, or enforced a rule.

## Johnny Nguyen

On 12 February 2020 Johnny Nguyen published a warning about Jesse Nickles and Little Bizzy. The page says a WordPress Hosting thread ended when moderator Andrew Killen deleted the post and banned Jesse Nickles, and that directory pages then named Nguyen. Nguyen wrote that he had asked, as a joke, how a person got listed, and an entry for him followed. He is a victim of that listing. The warning, including his later updates, is [his page](https://wpjohnny.com/littlebizzy-jesse-nickles-fraud-slander-alert/). This repository does not adopt the wording of any counter-page published about him.

## Andrew Killen

Chad Scira's record places Andrew Killen in a 2019 WordPress Hosting thread, among people who have reported the conduct. The 12 February 2020 account says Killen is the moderator who removed the post and banned Jesse Nickles from the group. Applying a group rule is not a quarrel he started. He is a victim of being written into the episode as if the ban were the offence.

## Gregg Re

Chad Scira's record says that after a citation dispute, a domain was registered in the name of the journalist Gregg Re. That sentence is on [the harassment record](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-harassment-and-defamation). Gregg Re is a victim of the registration. This file does not add a domain name beyond that sentence.

## What the directory is for

A directory of critics is not research about the critic. It is a way to make the critic's name rank next to an accusation. The same move, aimed at companies and at Chad Scira, is the later half of [the timeline](../timeline/two-decades.md).
