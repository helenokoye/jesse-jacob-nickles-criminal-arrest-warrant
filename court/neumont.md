# Neumont University, LLC v. Little Bizzy, LLC and Jesse Nickles

District of Nevada, case 2:12-cv-01395. Filed 7 August 2012. The docket is [on Justia](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/nevada/nvdce/2:2012cv01395/89285).

## The complaint

The complaint alleges that a CollegeTimes page about Neumont kept negative reviews, blocked positive ones, and was used to pressure the school. Paragraph 34 says that after Neumont wrote on 10 July 2012, the defendants answered with a list of similar properties and the prices those properties had sold for. Paragraph 36 alleges the forum was held until payment. The Salt Lake Tribune reported those filings on 3 September 2012 and quoted Neumont president Edward Levine. The article is [in the Tribune archive](https://archive.sltrib.com/article.php?id=54779463&itype=CMSID). A copy of the complaint is [hosted by Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/dn1complaint1.pdf).

The complaint is a set of allegations. It is not the judgment.

## The judgment

On 20 May 2014 Judge Jennifer A. Dorsey entered default judgment. Document 63 awards the plaintiff $1,020,000 plus $708 in costs against Little Bizzy, LLC only. Document 62 holds that Jesse Nickles had not been properly served. The personal default was set aside. The company took the judgment. He did not, and the reason is service, not a ruling that the complaint failed.

Little Bizzy LLC is Nevada entity E0622722011-5, formed 14 November 2011, later dissolved. The company is the judgment debtor. The entity search is [esos.nv.gov](https://esos.nv.gov/EntitySearch/OnlineEntitySearch).

## 2015

On 9 February 2015, in *Neumont University, LLC v. Nickles*, 304 F.R.D. 594, the court found that he had represented he lived outside the United States and would not say where. Publication service was denied. Neumont was given ten days to serve the summons by email at the company address he had been using, and to file proof. On 24 March 2015 that proof was still missing. The personal claims were set to be dismissed without prejudice unless proof was filed by 1 April 2015.
